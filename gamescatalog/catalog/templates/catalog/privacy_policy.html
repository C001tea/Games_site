Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GameVault ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you visit game-vault.dev.

We do not require registration and do not collect personal data directly. However, third-party services we use may collect data automatically:

Google Analytics — collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited, session duration, and general geographic location. This helps us understand how people use the site.

Google AdSense — may display personalized ads based on your browsing history using cookies. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies

We use cookies through Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Cookies are small files stored in your browser. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as expected.

Third-party links

GameVault contains links to external game stores (Steam, Fanatical, GOG, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you make a purchase through such a link, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Data retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Analytics data is retained by Google according to their own policies.

Contact
